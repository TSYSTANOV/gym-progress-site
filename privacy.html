Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information we collect

Gym Progress does not collect or store your personal workout data on our servers.

When you sign in with Google, the application may access:

  • Your Google account basic profile (name, email).
  • Your private Google Drive AppData folder.

Google Drive Backup

If backup is enabled, workout data is stored only inside your own Google Drive AppData folder. Only you can access these files.

The developer cannot read or access your backup.

Data Usage

  • No analytics.
  • No selling of personal information.
  • No sharing with third parties.
