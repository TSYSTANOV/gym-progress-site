🏋️ GYM Progress

GYM Progress is a mobile application for tracking workouts, monitoring exercise progress and securely backing up your data.

Features

  • Workout tracking
  • Exercise history
  • Statistics and charts
  • SQLite local storage
  • Optional Google Drive backup

Privacy

Your workout data is stored locally on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup, your backup is stored in your own private Google Drive AppData folder.

We do not collect, access or store your workout data.
